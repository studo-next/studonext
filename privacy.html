Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026 | Last Updated: 2026

At studonext (accessible from https://studo-next.github.io/studonext/), one of our primary commitments is the privacy and security of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of data collected, logged, and processed across our educational services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential details provided directly by users during interactive platform operations:

  • Counseling & Predictor Forms: Contact details, academic marks, and course preferences submitted to facilitate application routing.
  • Resume Engine Input: Personal summaries, education history, and technical competencies entered into the Resume Builder are processed client-side.
  • Marketplace Directory: Item titles, condition descriptions, and public contact information listed for book sales.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

studonext uses standard 'cookies' to store browser preferences and session metrics. These cookies optimize page load performance, retain temporary form states, and customize content based on browser technical specifications.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to site visitors based upon their visit to our platform and other sites across the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Visitors may choose to decline or opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Protection & Security

We deploy structural encryption and secure web script gateways (Firebase and Google API Endpoints) to protect user communications against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Children's Privacy Protection

studonext does not knowingly collect Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided personal information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately for prompt record removal.

6. Official Contact & Data Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our Privacy Policy or data security protocols, please reach out directly via contact@studonext.com or our Student Support Hub.
